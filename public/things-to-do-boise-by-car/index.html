Top Things to See in the Treasure Valley by Car

Scenic drives, landmarks, day trips and great places to eat, all with drive distances from Meridian, where the Corvette is based.

About the distances: Miles and drive times are approximate, measured from Meridian, Idaho in normal traffic. Use the Live directions links for exact, current routing from your own starting point. Check hours, seasonal closures and road conditions before you go.

Boise & close to Meridian

Idaho State Capitol

12 mi · ~20 min

A sandstone-and-marble landmark in the heart of downtown Boise, and one of the few capitols in the country heated with geothermal water. Great photo backdrop, easy downtown parking nearby.

Live directions

Basque Block

12 mi · ~20 min

Boise's Basque heritage on one walkable block: restaurants, the Basque Museum & Cultural Center and the Basque Market. Pair it with lunch or dinner downtown.

Live directions

Boise Depot & Platt Gardens

14 mi · ~22 min

A Spanish-style train depot on the bench with sweeping views straight down Capitol Boulevard to the Capitol dome. A classic golden-hour stop.

Live directions

Julia Davis Park & the Boise Greenbelt

13 mi · ~20 min

Downtown's biggest park, along the Boise River and Greenbelt. Rose Garden, zoo, museums and a great place to stretch your legs between drives.

Live directions

Old Idaho Penitentiary

16 mi · ~25 min

Idaho's territorial-era prison, now a museum, tucked into the sandstone foothills on Warm Springs Avenue. The drive in past the historic mansions is half the fun.

Live directions

Idaho Botanical Garden

16 mi · ~25 min

Themed gardens and foothill views right next to the Old Penitentiary, a good pairing for a relaxed half-day.

Live directions

Table Rock

17 mi · ~28 min

The sandstone bluff with the cross that overlooks the whole city. Drive up to the trailhead, then hike to the top for a valley-wide view (or just enjoy the winding approach).

Live directions

Hyde Park & Camel's Back Park

14 mi · ~22 min

Boise's North End: tree-lined streets, historic homes, cafes and boutiques on 13th Street, and the Camel's Back hill with a city overlook.

Live directions

Eagle Island State Park

12 mi · ~20 min

Riverside park with wide-open green space and a quick, easy drive from Meridian: a great low-key stop with the top down (or windows down).

Live directions

The Village at Meridian

3 mi · ~8 min

Meridian's open-air shopping and dining hub, a natural first stop to show off the car close to home.

Live directions

Scenic drives & day trips

Lake Lowell & Deer Flat National Wildlife Refuge

20 mi · ~30 min

A big reservoir near Nampa with flat, easy roads, birdwatching and sunset views. A relaxed cruise west of the Valley.

Live directions

Sunny Slope Wine Trail & Ste. Chapelle Winery

25 mi · ~30 min

Idaho's wine country on the hillside near Caldwell. Nice back roads, tasting rooms and valley views. (Please plan a designated driver if you're tasting.)

Live directions

Lucky Peak Reservoir & Discovery Park

25 mi · ~35 min

Follow ID-21 out of Boise to the dam and reservoir. A great first stretch of the Ponderosa Pine Scenic Byway with open sweepers.

Live directions

Bogus Basin Road

30 mi · ~50 min

The signature 'drive for driving's sake' near Boise: a 16-mile climb from the foothills to Bogus Basin with switchbacks and big views. Watch for cyclists, wildlife and mountain weather.

Live directions

Snake River Birds of Prey National Conservation Area

35 mi · ~50 min

Desert canyon country south of Kuna with one of the densest concentrations of nesting raptors in North America. Check road conditions first: some nearby roads are gravel, which isn't a good fit for a low sports car.

Live directions

Idaho City (Ponderosa Pine Scenic Byway)

52 mi · ~1 hr 10 min

A Gold Rush-era mountain town about 40 miles from Boise via ID-21. Boardwalks, old saloons, the historic cemetery and a very fun road to get there.

Live directions

Bruneau Dunes State Park

70 mi · ~1 hr 20 min

Home to the tallest single-structured sand dune in North America, plus an observatory. A straightforward I-84 drive with a desert payoff.

Live directions

Big road trips (half-day or more)

McCall & Payette Lake

110 mi · ~2 hr 5 min

Idaho's mountain-lake town via the Payette River Scenic Byway (ID-55). Our most popular day trip: see the full route guide.

Shoshone Falls (Twin Falls)

125 mi · ~2 hr

Known as the 'Niagara of the West' and taller than Niagara Falls. Best in spring runoff. Long freeway stretch, but an easy, quick highway drive.

Live directions

Sun Valley & Ketchum

150 mi · ~2 hr 45 min

Resort town at the foot of the Sawtooths via US-20 and the Sawtooth Scenic Byway (ID-75). See the full route guide.
